### How the clues work

Every guess must be a real word with the same number of letters as the answer. After each guess, every letter gets
a mark: a filled dot means the letter is in the right spot, a ring means the word has that letter somewhere else,
and a dash means it doesn't (or not that many times). The letter buttons keep track of what you have learned.

### About the words

Each day's word is a computing term that comes up in everyday life, from the cloud to cookies to chatbots. When the
puzzle ends, you'll see a short, plain explanation of what it means.
