Dr. Peilong Li is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at [Elizabethtown College](https://www.etown.edu/), where he has been on the faculty since 2018. He currently serves as the Program Director of the Computer Science and Data Science Programs. Prior to joining Etown, Dr. Li was a Research Assistant Professor at [University of Massachusetts Lowell](https://www.uml.edu/), where he also received his Ph.D. in Computer Engineering in 2015. His research applies machine learning to cybersecurity, including malware and network traffic analysis, edge AI and IoT systems, large language models and conversational agents, and privacy-preserving learning, with applications in health and education. He has received research grants from funding agencies including the National Science Foundation, Intel and Google, and his work has been cited over 2,000 times (Google Scholar h-index 15). He has also established the [Advanced Data Analytics Lab (ADAL)](https://bluejayadal.github.io/) for undergraduate student research at the college.
